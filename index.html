NES DEAL TRACKER

New complete-in-box (cart + box + manual) NES listings on Marktplaats, Tradera, eBay (.com, .de, .it, .co.uk) & 2dehands, checked against PriceCharting. Tap “All listings” to see loose and partial games too.

About the values. PriceCharting prices are a guide, not a quote. European (PAL) values come from thin sales data (often ~1 sale/month); US (NTSC) values are used for eBay and for US carts. Verdicts: Good buy ≥20% below the matching value, Fair within ±20%, Overpriced >20% above, Unclear when condition, game or price is unknown. Totals include shipping when known (Tradera shipping is the cheapest Swedish domestic rate; delivery to NL may differ). eBay.de / eBay.it / eBay.co.uk only show listings that ship to the Netherlands, with eBay's shipping quote to NL. For sellers outside the EU (e.g. UK sellers on eBay.co.uk) the total also includes 21% Dutch VAT, the EU's €3 flat duty per item (parcels up to €150, since 1 July 2026) and, from 1 November 2026, the €2 EU handling fee; above €150 a carrier clearance fee may come on top. 2dehands (Belgium) has no shipping to NL through the site, so those cards say “ask seller” and the verdict is on the asking price only. US/Canada imports on eBay.com may add ~21% VAT plus handling fees (not included).
